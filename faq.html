Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to solar plant installation, net-metering approvals, payback timelines, and subsidies.

Net metering allows your solar plant to export excess electricity generated during sunny daytime hours back to the local utility grid (e.g. UPPCL, DHBVN, BSES, MSEDCL). At the end of the monthly billing cycle, your DISCOM bill calculates only the net units consumed from the grid.
Commercial and industrial clients paying high grid tariffs (₹8.5 to ₹11 per unit) generally achieve complete financial payback in 3 to 3.5 years. Additionally, commercial enterprises benefit from 40% annual accelerated depreciation tax write-offs.
Under PM Surya Ghar, individual residential homeowners receive ₹30,000 per kW for 1 kW and 2 kW solar plants, plus ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW (Maximum subsidy ₹78,000). Housing societies (RWA) receive ₹18,000 per kW for common area lighting and elevator power.
