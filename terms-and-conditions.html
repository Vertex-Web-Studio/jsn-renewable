Terms & Conditions

Commercial terms, EPC project execution guidelines, and warranties of JSN Renewable Energy Private Limited.

1. Feasibility & Estimates

All solar plant sizing, power generation estimates, and payback periods generated by our online calculators are estimates based on standard solar irradiance. Final system engineering proposals are issued after an on-site structural and electrical shadow audit by JSN engineers.

2. DISCOM Net-Metering & Approvals

JSN Renewable Energy manages state power DISCOM net-metering applications and CEIG safety approvals on behalf of clients. Statutory approval timelines depend on local power DISCOM policies.

3. Equipment Warranty

Solar modules supplied by JSN feature 25-year performance warranties backed by Tier-1 ALMM listed manufacturers. Inverters carry standard 5 to 10-year manufacturer warranties.
