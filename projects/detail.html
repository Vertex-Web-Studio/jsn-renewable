2.55 MW PM-KUSUM Ground Solar Plant #1

2.55 MW • Bhiwani, Haryana

ENGINEERING & EXECUTION HIGHLIGHTS

Grid-Connected Utility Ground Solar Asset

Grid-connected MW-scale utility power plant engineered and commissioned under Component-A of federal PM-KUSUM program in Bhiwani, Haryana. The plant features ALMM listed mono-PERC bifacial solar modules, central inverters, 33kV outdoor substation, and a 25-year DISCOM Power Purchase Agreement (PPA).
