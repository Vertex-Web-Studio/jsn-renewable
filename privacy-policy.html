Privacy Policy

Data protection and privacy commitments of JSN Renewable Energy Private Limited.

1. Information Collection & Usage

JSN Renewable Energy Private Limited respects your privacy. When you request a site survey or submit an enquiry, we collect information such as name, phone number, email address, and site location solely to prepare engineering feasibility assessments and ROI proposals.

2. Visitor Telemetry & Cookie Preferences

We collect anonymized visitor IP addresses and browser telemetry for site security, regional performance logging, and load analysis. You can manage or decline non-essential cookies via our Cookie Consent banner at any time.

If you have any questions regarding our Privacy Policy, contact our Head Office at Wave City, Ghaziabad, UP or email us at info@jsnrenewable.com.
